# Skill: commercial-diligence

- **Purpose:** Structure market/customer/competitor diligence findings.
- **When to use:** Pre-IC diligence workstreams.
- **Required inputs:** Research scope + sources.
- **Available tools:** Web/research tools, deal-memory writer.
- **Expected outputs:** Findings log with source ratings + red flags.
- **Validation:** Every claim sourced; uncertainty labelled.
- **Approval requirements:** Draft low; external outreach high.
- **Failure modes:** Single-source critical claims → flag, don't assert.
