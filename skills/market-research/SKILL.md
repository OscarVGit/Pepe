# Skill: market-research

- **Purpose:** Repeatable market sizing and comp gathering.
- **When to use:** Thesis support, multiple benchmarking.
- **Required inputs:** Scope, approved sources list.
- **Available tools:** Web tools, OpenBB-style data providers (investigate).
- **Expected outputs:** Sourced tables + methodology note.
- **Validation:** Dates/units captured; stale sources flagged.
- **Approval requirements:** Draft low.
- **Failure modes:** Paywalled/uncertain data → cite limits explicitly.
